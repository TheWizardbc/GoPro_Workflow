GoPro Workflow

GoPro Workflow is a Windows desktop application for preparing GoPro MAX, HERO and Mission video content for Mapillary and Google Street View Studio.

The application validates and processes GPS data, prepares imagery and video files, and can publish validated Street View sequences to the Google account authorized by the user.

Google authorization is optional and is used only for Street View publishing and processing-status retrieval. GoPro Workflow does not receive or store the user's Google password.
